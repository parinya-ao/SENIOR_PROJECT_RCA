Microchip AVR128DA28/32/48/64 complete datasheet
- Current cited revision: DS40002183E, Rev. E (09/2024; ©2024), official PDF: https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MCU08/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/AVR128DA28-32-48-64-Data-Sheet-DS40002183.pdf
- DS40002183E Section 35.2.3 describes UPDI as a one-wire asynchronous half-duplex UART. Section 35.3.1.2 defines BREAK; its text says to send two consecutive BREAK characters to ensure receipt in all cases. Section 35.3.1.2.1 and Table 35-3 express one-wire BREAK timing in UPDI recovered-bit/prescaler terms, with worst-case recommended duration 24.60 ms at the slowest setting. This target-side timing basis must not be equated one-for-one with host UART baud bit periods. Under the logged 8E2 host format, an ideal zero byte has ten low host-bit periods (33.333 ms at 300 baud), nominally longer than 24.60 ms; this is not a measurement of actual low voltage/duration at the MCU.
- The E PDF and `pdftotext -layout` extract are copied from `evidence/RCA_PHY_01/target_path/` into this directory and included in SHA256SUMS. Their existing SHA-256 values are 0001076aa7fb795acef725a38e500e37efe01782b6328f02b54ac0dbb5482b3b (PDF) and fc64a7fd158361733c7d74e6cbac11d8395a1b14d5840a4ad8c3a9817f0f8bb8 (text).
