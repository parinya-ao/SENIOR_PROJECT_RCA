Source: Silicon Labs, CP2102/9 Data Sheet, Rev. 1.8 (Jan. 2017), Table 4 (UART and Suspend I/O DC Electrical Characteristics); VDD=3.0–3.6 V, -40 to +85 °C unless stated.
CP2102 TX output high: min VDD-0.1 V at IOH=-10 µA; min VDD-0.7 V at IOH=-3 mA; typical VDD-0.8 V at IOH=-10 mA.
CP2102 TX output low: max 0.1 V at IOL=10 µA; max 0.6 V at IOL=8.5 mA; typical 1.0 V at IOL=25 mA.
CP2102 UART input: VIH min 2.0 V; VIL max 0.8 V.
CP2102 regulator (Table 6): VDDOUT 3.0/3.3/3.6 V min/typ/max for 1–100 mA output current; maximum regulator supply current 100 mA.
CP2102 default USB descriptor Table 13: VID 10C4h, PID EA60h, serial 0001. This is configurable ROM data and does not authenticate actual silicon.
